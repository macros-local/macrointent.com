Delete your Macro Intent account

Takes effect straight away

In the app

  1. Open Macro Intent on your iPhone or Android phone.
  2. Tap More, then Delete account, and confirm.

On the web

  1. Sign in at app.macrointent.com.
  2. Open Settings, then select Delete account at the bottom of the page, and confirm.

Can’t sign in? Email macrointent@gmail.com from the address on your account and we’ll delete it for you.

What is deleted

Everything: your sign-in, profile and weigh-ins, targets, preferences, foods, recipes, weekly plans, meal photos and assistant conversation. Nothing is kept. Deleting an account can’t be undone. If you sign up again, you start from the beginning.
