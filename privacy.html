Privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026

Macro Intent plans meals against the calorie and macro targets you set, on the web at app.macrointent.com and in the iOS and Android apps. This page says what we keep, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it. We don’t sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

What we keep

WhatWhy
Your sign-in: email address, name, and the sign-in method you usedTo know which account is yours
Your profile: sex, age, height, weight and weigh-ins, activity, goal and paceTo suggest targets and show your progress
Your targets, preferences, foods, recipes and weekly plans, and what you log as eatenThis is the app
Photos of meals you addTo show them on your meals, and for the assistant to read when you send one in a chat
Your conversation with the assistantSo it can carry on a conversation; kept until you start a new chat

Activity and sleep

  • Steps, workouts and sleep from Apple Health or Health Connect are read only if you allow it. The app keeps the last two weeks’ step counts, the workouts you count, and each night’s hours and sleep stages on our servers, so they show on your days, in your reports and to a coach you’ve accepted. Edits you make to steps or workouts stay on your phone. We never write to Apple Health or Health Connect, and health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone else.
  • Oura and Ultrahuman: if you connect a ring, we keep the sign-in it gives us and read your nightly sleep score. The sign-in stays on our servers and is never sent to the app. Disconnect it in Settings → Sleep at any time.

Pinterest

If you connect Pinterest, we keep the sign-in Pinterest gives us (on our servers only), the boards you choose and the meal type you give each, and which pins we’ve already read. We read only those boards’ pins, never post or change anything on Pinterest, and turn each new pin into a recipe of yours. To read a pin we open the page it links to, and may send its description or picture to Anthropic (below) to read the recipe written there. Disconnect in Customize → Pinterest; the recipes already brought in stay until you delete them.

Coaching

If you accept a coach, they can see your meals, totals, targets, weigh-ins, steps, workouts and sleep, read-only; they can change your targets and leave notes on your days, and nothing else. They see your name and email, and you see theirs. Either of you can end it at any time in Coaching. We keep notices in the app (a coach asking to connect, a note, new Pinterest recipes) until you delete your account.

What stays on your device

  • Voice: when you speak to the assistant, your phone’s or browser’s own speech recognition (from Apple, Google or your browser’s maker) turns it into text. We receive only the text.
  • Barcodes are read by the camera on your phone. Only the barcode number is sent, to look up the food.

The assistant and AI

The assistant is powered by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic. When you use it, what you type or say, photos you add, and the parts of your plan, targets and foods it needs are sent to Anthropic to produce a reply. Anthropic processes them on our behalf and does not use them to train its models. The apps ask before the first message. Macros and amounts are always worked out by our own code, not the AI.

Who else handles your data

Only these services, each to run part of Macro Intent:

  • Auth0 (Okta): sign-in
  • MongoDB Atlas: our database
  • Amazon Web Services: hosting
  • Anthropic: the assistant, when you use it, and reading recipes from pins you choose
  • Pinterest, Oura, Ultrahuman: only if you connect them, to read the boards or sleep scores you ask for
  • Open Food Facts: food and barcode lookups. These lookups contain the search or barcode only, nothing about you.

We share data with no one else, except where the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep your data until you delete your account. You can delete it at any time in the app (You → Settings → Delete account) or on the web (Settings → Delete account). That removes your plans, recipes, foods, photos, settings, activity and sleep, connections, coaching links, assistant conversation and sign-in from our systems straight away. More on deleting your account.

Your rights

You can see and change almost everything we keep in the app itself. To ask for a copy of your data, or about anything on this page, email us at macrointent@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights to access, correct, move or delete your data, which we will honour.

Children

Macro Intent is not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly keep data about them.

Not medical advice

Targets and suggestions are estimates for general wellness. They are not medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and its date, and tell you in the app if the change matters.
