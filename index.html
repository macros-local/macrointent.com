A week that adds up

Pick your goal and how you like to eat. Every day is built to land inside your targets, with portions worked out by real arithmetic, not guesses.

Log it your way

Snap a photo, scan a barcode, or just say what you ate. The assistant fits it into your day and rebalances the rest.

Shop and prep in one go

The week becomes a grocery list sorted by aisle, and a prep plan that says what to cook, assemble or pull from the fridge each day.

Your recipes, your foods

Import a recipe from a link, add your own, or use the recipe bank. Tell it the milk and protein you buy and every recipe follows.

Insights, not lectures

See when sugar runs high or a day comes up short, with a one-tap swap or a better dinner that fixes it.

Moves with you

Steps from Apple Health or Health Connect sit next to what you eat, so active days get the food they need.
