Logging

One button. Four ways to log.

Tap + and pick the meal. However you'd rather tell it, the numbers come from real food data, never a guess.

Photo

Snap the plate, or choose up to four from your library: the meal, the label, the side. You see what it found before anything is saved.

Say it

“Two eggs, toast and a flat white.” It turns into foods and amounts, fitted to your day.

Search

Your recent foods first, then your own, every USDA ingredient and the brands you buy. Add several, save once.

Ask

“What should dinner be to finish on protein?” The assistant suggests, you decide. It never changes your plan on its own.

Everything else

A plan that adds up, all week.

Macro Intent does the arithmetic so you can just cook and eat.

A week that hits your targets

Each day is built from recipes you like and scaled to land inside your calories and macros, by a real solver rather than a guess.

Grocery list and prep plan

The week becomes a list sorted by aisle, scaled for your household, and a plan for what to cook, assemble or pull from the fridge.

Recipes from anywhere

A link, a photo of a cookbook page, your Pinterest boards, or the recipe bank. Every ingredient matched to real nutrition data.

Insights, not lectures

When sugar runs high or protein comes up short, you get a one-tap swap or a better dinner that fixes the day.

Steps, workouts and sleep

From Apple Health or Health Connect, with an Oura or Ultrahuman sleep score if you wear one. Active days get the food they need.

Bring your coach

Share your week with a coach. They see your meals and progress, set your targets and leave notes on your days.

New · Pinterest

Pin it on Friday. Eat it on Monday.

Connect Pinterest and give each board a meal: “Work lunches” for lunch, “Breakfast ideas” for breakfast. New pins come in as recipes with real nutrition, ready to plan.

  • Reads the recipe from the pin’s page, its description, or the recipe written on the picture
  • Each one waits for your ✓, or turn on auto-add and they go straight in
  • Change the meal type, edit amounts or reject, right from the card
